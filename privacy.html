Privacy Policy

ONA Privacy Policy

ONA · Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dap-itech ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the ONA mobile application ("the App"). By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1Summary

  • The App does not currently require an account or sign-in. If a future version adds sign-in, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.
  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information on our servers.
  • Your learning progress stays on your device.
  • The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • The App uses your microphone only to recognize the words you speak during exercises.

2Information Stored on Your Device

To save your progress, the App stores the following in local storage on your device:

  • Selected language and regional setting
  • Daily learning goal
  • XP, streak, completed units, and game stage progress
  • Daily study time

This information is never transmitted to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings.

3Microphone and Speech Recognition

The App requests microphone permission so you can practice speaking. Audio is used only to recognize the word or phrase you say, so the App can tell you whether your answer matched the expected word. We do not record, store, or share your audio.

Speech recognition is provided by the speech service built into your Android device, typically provided by Google. That service may process audio under its own privacy policy and terms. You can deny or revoke microphone permission at any time in your Android settings. The App remains usable, and speaking exercises offer a skip option.

4Text-to-Speech

The App uses your device's text-to-speech engine to read words and phrases aloud. No personal information is involved.

5Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics through the App.

6How We Use Information

Because we do not receive your information, we do not use it. The data described in Section 2 is used only inside the App to show your progress and to run lessons and games.

7Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information. The App contains no third-party advertising or analytics services. The 3D graphics library used by the App is bundled inside the App and does not contact external servers.

8Data Retention

Progress data remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. We do not retain any data on our servers because we do not receive it.

9Your Choices and Rights

  • Reset progress: Use the Menu inside the App to erase all saved data.
  • Microphone: Revoke permission in Android settings at any time.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on your device.

Because we do not hold your personal information, requests for access, correction, or deletion can be handled directly on your device. If you are located in a region with specific privacy rights, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you may contact us as described below to exercise those rights.

10Children's Privacy

The App is designed for language learning and is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13. The App does not show advertising, does not use analytics, and does not collect personal information, so we do not collect personal information from children. Children can use the App without providing any personal details.

Parents and guardians can review the App's content and features before allowing a child to use it, and can delete the App's data at any time from the Menu inside the App or by uninstalling the App. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

11Security

Because your progress is stored only on your device, its security depends largely on your device's security. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with a screen lock.

12International Use

Because the App does not transmit personal data to us, there is no cross-border transfer of personal information by us. Speech recognition may be processed by your device's speech service according to that service's terms.

13Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be reflected in an App update where appropriate. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
